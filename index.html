I’m Ismail Dawjee, a product and experience designer in Johannesburg. I design from the business model to the customer moments that matter most, and these days I dare to build software too.

CPTO · Immersion X MD · Immersion Group Judge · FNB App of the Year 2026
what I believe

Design is how a good intention is materialised, delivered and iterated.

I don’t draw a line between the business model, the revenue and the customer. To me they’re one thing, and that’s where the best experiences, sustainable businesses and real impact get made.

Design from the model to the moment.

At IBM I designed end-to-end customer journeys for some of the largest deals in the Middle East and Africa, putting Watson inside a frequent flyer’s day or a pet insurer’s renewal. Then I became one of the first members of the IBM iX team in South Africa.

Nedbank · Digital Fast Lane

Inside a bank, the constraints got real. MobiMoney put a full account on a USSD menu for people without smartphones. Stokvel took a savings club built on trust and made it digital without breaking the trust. MoneyApp let people open an account with their face.

Nedbank · RBB Digital
